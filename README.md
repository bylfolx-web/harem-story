# The Repairman

A dark, literary adult romance set in the Sailor Moon universe, post-manga. Working title: **The Repairman**.

This repo is the story's bible and save state — the reference material a writing session needs to pick up cold and keep continuity straight across chapters. Prose chapters live outside it; what's tracked here is canon, craft rules, and what's already been fired.

---

## Premise

Three years ago something ancient and motiveless drifted in from the dark between galaxies and ate love and light. It took Haruka first. After three weeks of cat-and-mouse across the country, Saturn put her down far enough for Michiru to finish it — she murdered her own lover — and in the creature's one vulnerable moment Usagi spent herself to kill it. The Senshi survived. Their princess didn't.

Now they're nineteen to twenty-one and scattered into ordinary lives: a shrine going to rot, a med-school sprint, three jobs and a flower shop fund, a condo full of light and a violin, an idol contract, a college quad.

Into that walks **Byl** — 29, six-four, unkempt, broke, sarcastic, a maintenance man known in the trade for jury-rigging anything with a pulse. Yakuza killed his mother and sisters ten years ago; he quit the cello the same week. He fixes their chillers, their roofs, their grills, one at a time, and has no idea why the dreams won't stop.

He was **Orion**, first blade of the Silver Millennium's personal guard, sent past the gates with his dog Lucille to be the first line against whatever came. He failed against Metallia. Queen Serenity carved him out of all five Senshi's memories before she sent him — clean-edged, one-man-and-one-dog shaped holes in every one of them.

The story is the slow return of that, from five directions at once.

## Cast at a glance

**Byl** — 29. The repairman. Blunt, broke, good with his hands. Golden retriever named **Lucy**: no leash, no collar, waits outside fence lines, trained bite-hold, ten years old this winter with no gray on the muzzle at all. Nobody's asked the right question about that yet.

**Rei Hino / Mars** — 19. Shrine, ¥61.4M inheritance, six weeks of repairs booked as a standing daily reason for him to be there. Has seen the throne room in the fire. Knows he existed, knows he was erased, believes he's dead. Has not connected him to Byl.

**Michiru Kaioh / Neptune** — 21. Violinist, condo full of light, Hotaru living with her. Met him once on a roof; he fixed the fourth section of the Vasarhelyi in one sentence and left. Considering Kurosawa Jun out of open vanity. *"Twice is a coincidence. Three times is a fact."*

**Hotaru Tomoe / Saturn** — 19. University, no major, trying to build a social life on purpose. Traded numbers over a dead tennis ball. The easiest door in the house.

**Ami Mizuno / Mercury** — 19. Rebuilt a rooftop unit beside him for two hours and never got his name. Wants to take his brain apart.

**Makoto Kino / Jupiter** — 19. Three jobs, flower shop fund, bleeding money into her boyfriend's diner. Ventilation problem incoming via Rei's referral.

**Minako Aino / Venus** — 19. Idol track. Jumped into an alley fight in a cocktail dress without transforming, butterflied his scalp at a gas station, and took his number as payment.

## Working notes

- The Craft and Style Bible governs. Plain anatomical vocabulary, Sanderson-grade concreteness on settings, no hypophora, no "not X but Y," never close on a question.
- NPCs are real. They lie, scheme, and refuse; the author's voice doesn't leak into them.
- Continuity lives in the save states. When a chapter closes, log the deltas — what's now known, by whom, and what's been spent.
- Content: adult fiction, explicit, dark themes throughout. All characters are adults.
